Home to roughly 500 family households, our ward offers the ideal balance between quiet, rustic Nagaland living and accessible community facilities.

The Historic Hospital:
From Healing Center to Living Quarters

In the earlier decades of Tuli's development, the old hospital building in Longkongmendang served as a vital medical lifeline for residents across Mile Tuli and neighboring settlements.

Although medical operations transitioned to newer regional health centers over time, the colony council chose to carefully preserve the vintage wooden and brick architecture. Today, rather than lying dormant, the building has been thoughtfully repurposed as residential living quarters for rented accommodation—offering character, history, and home to resident families.
